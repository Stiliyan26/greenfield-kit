# PartyFox product context

Status: working notes from the 9 September call, supplied by Stiliyan. This is not a signed client brief. PartyFox already pays a third party CRM vendor; check what it has delivered before proposing replacement work.

PartyFox runs children's parties. The owner moves one booking through incoming site order, scheduling, animator assignment, parent message, external providers, deposit check, party day, feedback, and month end pay. Today this information is repeated across email, Google Calendar, Viber, bank checks, and forms.

Confirmed needs from the notes:

- Site orders arrive in one inbox.
- Each animator sees correct party times and a usable address.
- Animators request leave; the owner approves it.
- Owner and animator can see when a deposit is missing, with a clear red warning.
- The owner can find customer history, repeat families, large orders, and upcoming birthdays.
- Animators can see their own pay so month end checking takes less manual work.

Owner ideas, not confirmed client requirements: automatic animator choice, one click Viber sending, AI drafted birthday messages, and one click subcontractor requests.

Unknown: what the paid vendor shipped, real site form fields, deposit rule, animator cut, bonus formula, permission details, Google sync, Viber API feasibility, and corporate party flow. Show unknown figures as unset. Use fake names and sample bookings in local prototypes.
